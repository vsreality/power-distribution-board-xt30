VSReality Robotics - PD-XT30-5 v1.0

Editable KiCad 9 project, PCB and matching schematic.
Layout follows the supplied sketch: input at the top, four outputs below,
all in one column, with an M3 mounting hole at each end.

MECHANICAL
Board: 18 x 52 mm; 4 mm corner radii approximated by short outline segments.
Connector bodies are centred on the board width; equal 7 mm row pitch.
Front silkscreen: ports 1-5 and polarity. Back also carries VSReality Robotics,
PD-XT30-5 and v1.0.
Thickness: 1.6 mm including solder mask. FR4, two copper layers.
Mounting: 2 x 3.2 mm finished NON-PLATED holes for M3 screws.
Hole centres: (9,5) and (9,47) mm from the board's top-left envelope.
Mounting pitch: 42 mm. Copper keepout diameter: 7 mm on both sides.
Use screw heads/washers no larger than 6 mm OD to retain a 0.5 mm margin.
Connector body centres: x=9 mm, y=12,19,26,33,40 mm from top-left.
Connector pitch down the board: 7 mm. Pin pitch: 5 mm.
Male-body nominal gap between rows is 1.8 mm; footprint courtyard gap is
0.8 mm. Physical access with the actual mating cables remains to be checked.
Connector holes: 10 x 1.8 mm finished plated holes; standard 3 mm pads.

COPPER / FABRICATION
Specify 70 um (2 oz) finished copper on BOTH layers when ordering.
Both layers: V+ LEFT strip as viewed from the FRONT; GND fills all remaining
available area, including the right side and both end areas.
V+ zone: x=0..8.75 mm, y=10..42 mm; 0.5 mm clearance to surrounding GND.
V+ is excluded from the full 10 mm mounting region at EACH end. The existing
7 mm diameter screw/washer keepouts remain copper-free on both layers.
A rear view mirrors left and right. Same-net plated connector pads join
the corresponding copper between layers.
Planes fill their assigned areas except necessary clearance around opposite-net
pads, board edges and mounting hardware. No thermal spokes or narrow traces.
Minimum copper clearance: 0.5 mm. Copper-to-edge clearance: 0.5 mm.
Solder mask both sides; white silkscreen both sides; lead-free HASL finish.
Solid plane connections require sufficient soldering heat/preheat.
Gerber and separate PTH/NPTH drill files are in fabrication/.
Gerbers alone do not specify copper thickness: explicitly select 2 oz per side.

CONNECTORS / BOM (PROVISIONAL GENDER)
J1: quantity 1, AMASS XT30UPB-F, vertical PCB connector, port 1.
J2-J5: quantity 4, AMASS XT30UPB-M, vertical PCB connectors, ports 2-5.
H1-H2: M3 mounting hardware, length selected for the enclosure/standoffs.
Standard KiCad Connector_AMASS footprints are embedded in the PCB.
This design is for PCB-tail XT30UPB parts, not solder-cup XT30 cable parts.
Top view: positive on LEFT, negative on RIGHT, matching the reference sketch.
Pin 1 = GND; pin 2 = V+. All five connectors are wired in parallel.
Check actual connector housing polarity before soldering.
Input female / outputs male was an unconfirmed working assumption. Select
the mating gender appropriate to the existing harness before assembly;
recessed female contacts are normally preferred on energized supply cables.

ELECTRICAL LIMITS STILL TO CONFIRM
Operating voltage and total continuous current were not provided.
No validated current or voltage rating is claimed for this board.
The input carries the SUM of all four output currents. Connector, solder joint,
wire, ambient-temperature and cooling limits apply even with broad planes.
The board contains no fuse, reverse-polarity protection or voltage regulation.
Provide source and branch protection appropriate to the connected wiring.
Check continuity, isolation and polarity before applying power. Validate the
assembled unit's voltage drop and temperature rise at the intended total load.

VERIFICATION
KiCad 9.0.6 DRC: zero violations, zero unconnected pads, zero schematic parity
issues. Schematic ERC report is included. Bare-board previews are retained.
The assembled 3D view includes one XT30UPB-F and four XT30UPB-M models from
realthunder, stored in models/ with project-relative paths. Models use a
2.5 mm X offset to align their centred origin with the footprint pin-1 origin.
In KiCad 3D Viewer, enable through-hole models if they are hidden.
See models/MODEL-SOURCES.txt for provenance. Physical fit and load testing
have not been performed. The PCB and manufacturing output remain review drafts
until the connector choice and intended voltage/current are established.
